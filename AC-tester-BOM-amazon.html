Passive piezo buzzer (through-hole) LS1 1 Audible state indicator (a distinct tone per state, plus a startup jingle and an error tone). The board drives it through Q4, so it must be a passive piezo transducer (not an active module). JESSINIE 1407, 14x7 mm, 7.5 mm pitch (fits the 7.6 mm LS1 holes), ~4 kHz. Pitch is the binding spec, so avoid the 12 mm models (1207/1225/12085) in this family, they are 6.5 mm pitch and will not line up. The LS1 footprint is a 15 mm outline that covers the 14 mm body, placed clear of the terminal block. Exact 7.6 mm-pitch drop-in is the TDK PS1440P02BT (DigiKey), not on Amazon. Buy
SW1 / SW2 are headers, not the buttons. The board has 2-pin headers for MODE and SELECT. The actual panel buttons are off-board (section 4) and wire to these headers. If you only bench-test, you can short the header pins by hand instead.

Power: pick one source, not both. Either plug a 5 V adapter into the J2 barrel jack, or power the XIAO over its own USB-C. The barrel feeds the AP2112K LDO (placed by JLCPCB), which makes 3.3 V and back-feeds the XIAO's 3V3 pad. Do not feed both at once.
